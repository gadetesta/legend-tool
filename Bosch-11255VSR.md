# Bosch 11255VSR 1" Rotary Hammer

**Manufacturer:** Bosch  
**Model:** 11255VSR  
**Size:** 1"  
**Tool type:** Rotary hammer

A rotary hammer is a heavy-duty power tool that uses a piston-driven mechanism to pound and spin the drill bit simultaneously, making it ideal for drilling and chiseling hard masonry like concrete and brick.
